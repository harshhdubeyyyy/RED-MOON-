# Dedicated to Teju — Jal Pari

The Jal Pari Language is dedicated to Tejaswini, whom Jayant calls
"Jal Pari".

Not a grand gesture, just a simple one, in her own words' spirit:
simple and friendly, with an open door and zero pressure.

> For Jal Pari — may your days be as bright as a Goa sunset,
> and may the code you inspire always run clean and true.

— Jayant Dubey, 26 Oct 2026

The language was formerly called Forge. Its technical names
(`forge.py`, `forge_lang`, `.fg` files, and the RED-MOON- repo)
are unchanged for compatibility, so existing programs keep
working. Only the name — and the dedication — are new.
